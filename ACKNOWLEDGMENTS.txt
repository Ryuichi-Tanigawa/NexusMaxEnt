Scientific Background and Acknowledgments

1. Scientific Background & Pioneering Works
This software is informed by and built upon published research in maximum-entropy 
reconstruction, constrained optimization, inverse Laplace transformation, and 
DOSY NMR analysis.

The author gratefully acknowledges Dr. Marc-André Delsuc and the developers and 
contributors of GIFA and PALMA for their pioneering scientific work in maximum-entropy 
NMR reconstruction and advanced data processing algorithms. 

Special acknowledgment is also given to Dr. John Skilling and co-workers for their 
foundational contributions to the maximum entropy method in spectroscopy and data analysis.

Key foundational literature includes:
- PALMA: Cherni, A., Chouzenoux, E., & Delsuc, M. A. (2017). PALMA, an improved 
  algorithm for DOSY signal processing. Analyst, 142(5), 772-779.
- GIFA: Pons, J.-L., Malliavin, T. E., & Delsuc, M. A. (1996). Gifa V. 4: A complete 
  package for NMR data set processing. Journal of Biomolecular NMR, 8(4), 445-452. 
  https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00228146
- Classic MaxEnt: Skilling, J. (1989). Classic Maximum Entropy. In Maximum Entropy 
  and Bayesian Methods (pp. 45-52). Springer, Dordrecht.

2. Open-Source Ecosystem & Tools
This software relies heavily on the vibrant Python open-source community and the 
following foundational tools and libraries:

- Python Language & Runtime: Core programming environment.
- NumPy & SciPy: Essential libraries for high-performance numerical computing 
  and scientific algorithms.
- CuPy: GPU-accelerated array computing for high-speed matrix processing.
- Matplotlib: Data visualization and spectral plotting.
- Cython & Setuptools: Performance optimization and build management.
- PyInstaller: Packaging into standalone executable distribution.
- Miniconda & conda-forge: Environment management and package distribution.

The author expresses sincere gratitude to all maintainers, developers, and 
contributors who make these invaluable tools available to the global scientific 
and technical community.
